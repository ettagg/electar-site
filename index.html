How a paper works

  1. Place the scene

    ARCore sets it on the floor or a wall. On phones without ARCore, a printed marker card holds it in place on the desk.

  2. Make the call

    Three buttons sit beside three different objects: the breaker, the tester, the tape. Every wrong option is wrong for a reason the author wrote down, and the app plays it out before that reason appears.

  3. Watch the step happen

    The breaker opens, the travellers land, current runs along the conduit. The buttons step aside while it plays, then the next decision appears beside its own objects.

Points per decision

Right on the first try10
Right after one wrong choice5
Right after both wrong choices0
  • A decision never drops below zero, and picking the same wrong option twice costs nothing more.
  • Repositioning the scene keeps every mistake, so it can’t be used to start clean.
  • Pass mark for an AR paper: 70%.

      The rest of the spec

      Supply
      230 V, 60 Hz, single phase, on two lines. With L1 and L2 both live, an OFF wall switch doesn’t make a lamp safe. Only the 2-pole breaker does, which is why both switching samples open with it.
      Wire and breakers
      2.0 mm² is the minimum; plans call for 3.5 mm² THHN. 15 A breakers on 2.0 mm² lighting circuits, 20 A on 3.5 mm² outlet circuits.
      Raceway
      15 mm (½″) at the smallest. Orange PVC conduit on saddles, or white PVC moulding.
      Switch names
      3-way switches control a lamp from two places (S3); a 4-way adds a third (S4). These are the DepEd and TESDA names, so the app never contradicts the module.
      Language
      English or Filipino. Technical terms stay in English, the way they’re said on the job and in the NC II assessment.
