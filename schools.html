Questions

Before you write

What phones do students need?

An Android phone. On phones with ARCore, scenes are placed on the classroom floor or a wall. Phones without it still work: the app comes with a marker card to print, and the scene sits on the card.

Do teachers need to install anything?

No. The teacher’s web desk runs in the browser on any laptop or desktop.

Does it follow the EIM curriculum?

Yes. Lessons are filed by quarter for Senior High TVL Electrical Installation and Maintenance, and the wiring follows Philippine Electrical Code practice. You can see the full year on the showcase.

Is it in Filipino?

Students can switch the app to Filipino. Teachers can add a Filipino version of any quiz question.

How big is the app?

About 300 MB for the current test build. It’s worth downloading once on school Wi-Fi.

Can I try it before I write?

Yes. Play a full sample in your browser, or download the Android test build from the showcase.
